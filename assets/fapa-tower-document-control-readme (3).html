FAPA Tower Document Control

Built with the help of AI for 43 Air School — an operational records system for Port Alfred Tower, replacing paper logs with a single accountable digital register.

What it is

A single, signed-off record for everything that happens in the Tower

Port Alfred Tower needed one accountable place to log who was on duty, when, doing what — and to keep that record provable after the fact. Attendance, weekly duty rosters, training sign-offs, license checks, and equipment faults all generate paperwork that someone, eventually, has to be able to point to and say "here's proof this happened, and here's who signed off on it."

This system replaces that scattered paper trail with one register, gated by role, where every entry carries a timestamp, a name, and — where it matters — a digital signature.

What it tracks

Six registers, one system

Each module is its own logbook, but they all sit inside the same app, under the same accountability model.

01
Attendance
Daily register
Staff clock in and out, log breaks, and sign their own daily record. Admins can review and countersign entries, so there's a clear chain of who confirmed what.
02
Duties
Weekly roster
Recurring duty assignments are generated automatically each week, tracked through to completion, and can be handed over between staff. Overdue or missed duties trigger a notification rather than going quietly unnoticed.
03
Training Files
Per-trainee record
Each trainee has a running file of debriefs and structured assessments. Entries are editable until signed off, after which they're locked and exportable as a formal PDF training record.
04
License Checks
Compliance record
Periodic license checks are logged per staff member and exportable on demand, giving admins a clear compliance history without digging through paper files.
05
Unserviceability Log
Equipment faults
Faults are reported as they happen, tracked month to month if unresolved, and closed out with a signed fix record. A monthly sign-off gives the Officer in Charge a clean, exportable log.
06
User Management
Admin only
Admins approve new accounts and manage who has access to what — keeping the register's integrity tied to a controlled list of real people.
How it's built with the help of AI

Stack & approach

Built as a lightweight, dependency-light web app rather than a heavy framework project — fast to load on a Tower desktop, and cheap to host and maintain long-term.

Frontend
Vanilla JS & CSS
A modular, hand-built single-page app — no UI framework — with client-side rendering and a printable-document layer for exports.
Backend / Data
Supabase
Postgres database with authentication and role-based access, so the data layer enforces who can see and sign what — not just the UI.
Hosting
Netlify
Continuous deployment straight from the source repository, with environment-managed secrets kept out of version control.
Notifications
Automated email
Missed duties, reported faults, and fixed faults all trigger an email to the relevant staff — no one has to go looking for the news.
Entry is made
A staff member clocks in, completes a duty, writes a debrief, or reports a fault — each tied to their authenticated identity.
Sign-off where it matters
Training entries, duty completions, and snag fixes carry a digital signature before they're considered closed.
Relevant people are notified
Missed duties and open faults are emailed automatically, so accountability doesn't depend on someone remembering to check.
Record becomes exportable
Every register can be pulled as a clean, print-ready PDF — the same document trail a paper logbook would have produced, without the paper.
