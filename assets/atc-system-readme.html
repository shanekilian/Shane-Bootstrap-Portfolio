What it is

A single platform replacing a stack of paper and spreadsheets

43 Air School runs an air traffic control training program with students, instructors, administrators, and an IT/maintenance function, each generating their own paperwork — lesson debriefs, training files, leave requests, equipment fault logs, mock exam papers, flight plan messages, and booking calendars. Before this system, that workflow lived across paper forms, email threads, and disconnected spreadsheets.

The platform brings all of it into one application with a single source of truth, role-specific dashboards, and automatic email notifications, so the right person is notified the moment something needs their attention rather than someone having to chase it down.

Who uses it

Four roles, four different views of the same system

Rather than one generic interface, each person signs in to a dashboard scoped to exactly what their role needs — nothing more.

Student
Trainee pilots
  • Views their own lesson debriefs and signs off on completed ones
  • Books and views upcoming training sessions on a shared calendar
  • Submits leave applications for review
  • Practises with auto-marked mock exam papers built from instructor-uploaded question sets
  • Sends test messages through a simulated AFTN flight-plan system
  • Completes assigned feedback / assessment forms
Instructor
Teaching staff
  • Writes and manages lesson debriefs for their students, with structured scoring against lesson objectives
  • Maintains the authorisation sheet (sign-on/sign-off log) per lesson
  • Manages bookings and the training calendar
  • Reviews and completes ATS/ATO assessment forms
  • Can optionally hold a dual student role for their own ongoing training
Admin
School management
  • Approves new account requests and manages active users and permissions
  • Generates consolidated training files per student as exportable PDFs
  • Oversees the master autho sheet, mock question bank, and booking calendar across all students
  • Reviews and decides on leave requests, with a generated decision letter
  • Has full visibility over debriefs and feedback forms school-wide
IT
Maintenance / support
  • Logs and resolves equipment faults through an unserviceability log, with month-end status tracking and sign-off
  • Manages the simulator booking schedule
How it's built with the help of AI

Stack & architecture

A single-page web application, built with the help of AI to be lightweight to deploy and cheap to run, while still handling real authentication, file storage, and role permissions properly rather than faking them on the front end.

Frontend
Vanilla JS, HTML & CSS
No framework — a single-page app with client-side view routing, modal system, and a responsive design system built with the help of AI from scratch.
Backend / Data
Supabase
Postgres database, authentication, row-level security for role permissions, and file storage for uploaded documents and subject materials.
Notifications
EmailJS
Automated email notifications for approvals, assigned feedback forms, overdue tasks, and equipment fault reports.
Documents
Generated PDFs
Training files, leave decision letters, and unserviceability logs are generated as print-ready, exportable PDF documents.
